Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026

This policy explains what information The World Forgets collects when you play it, why, and the choices you have. It applies to The World Forgets as distributed on Google Play (package com.bytelogixs.theworldforgets). The World Forgets is made by Byte Logixs ("we", "us").

  • You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your journey, the journal pages you have found and your settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The app can show a short video ad, but only when you choose to watch one for a hint. Those ads come from Google AdMob, which collects device information as described below.
  • You can play the whole game without watching an ad.

1. Information stored on your device

The World Forgets saves your place in the journey, the rooms you have passed, the journal pages you have found and your settings (such as sound and the size and position of the touch buttons) in the app's private storage on your device.

To help us fix problems, the game also keeps a small log on your device of what happens while you play, such as which room you entered and when you restored an object, along with any error the game runs into. This log is capped at about 2 MB, contains nothing that identifies you, and is never sent to us.

None of this information leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by choosing New game on the title screen (which replaces your saved journey), by clearing the app's data in your device's Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Rewarded video ads

When you ask for a hint, The World Forgets offers to show a short video. The video plays only if you choose to watch it, and the hint appears once the video has finished. If you decline, you simply carry on playing.

These videos are provided by Google AdMob, an advertising service of Google. To choose, show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and processes information such as:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Advertising ID);
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (such as country or city);
  • device and app information, such as the device model, operating system version, language, app version and network type;
  • how you interact with ads, such as whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped;
  • crash and performance information about the ads SDK.

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your advertising identifier or any other personal information from these ads. We only see combined reports, such as how many ads were shown. To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your consent and choices

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, The World Forgets shows Google's consent form. You can accept or decline personalised ads, and change your choice later with Privacy choices on the title screen.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact menu name varies by device).
  • Everywhere: you can simply decline whenever a video is offered. No ad is shown unless you ask for a hint.

4. Children

The World Forgets is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share information about you with anyone, except that Google AdMob receives the information described in section 2 when the app requests an ad.

6. Security and retention

Ad requests are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The progress and logs saved on your device stay there until you erase them or remove the app. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to or limit how it is used. Because we don't hold personal information about you, most of these requests are handled by the controls above or by Google's privacy tools. You can also contact us with any question.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Google Play listing. If a future version of the game adds optional sign-in, this policy will be updated to describe it before that version is released.
